About Crash

Collectors know collectors.

I'm Christopher "Crash" Payton — a lifelong collector based in Tillsonburg, Ontario. What started as a personal passion for Funko Pops, retro games and rare finds has evolved into a full resale operation built on trust, condition, and fair pricing.

I source from conventions, estate sales, and wholesale channels — and I only sell what I'd be proud to own. When you buy from Crash's Collectables, you're buying from someone who genuinely cares about this hobby.
